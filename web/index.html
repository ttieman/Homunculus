  1. Play the whole clip. Check the arms, legs and body direction.
  2. Compare with the source recording when available.
  3. Look for jumps or cropped limbs before editing.

BODY TARGETS & CLIP MIXER

Choose what moves

Choose individual joints

Only selected local joint tracks are exported in isolation mode. Other parts stay in their rest pose here and can be driven by another animation in your game. Children still follow moving parents.

Layering fits the second clip to the base duration. Joining overlaps the clips by the transition time; unselected joints hold the base's final pose after it ends. These operations do not solve foot contacts.
